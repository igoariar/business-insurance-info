# Backlog MVP - WordPress (Free budget)

Target duration: 6 weeks (weekly sprints). Audience: general public, daily updates.

Sprint 0 — Curation & scope (2-3 days)
- Define sections: News, Guides, How-tos, Practical sheets, Simulators.
- Editorial charter (plain English tone), content templates (post, guide, tool).
- 30-day editorial skeleton.

Sprint 1 — WordPress setup & hosting (1 week)
- Choose free/low-cost hosting: WordPress.com free tier or a low-cost shared host trial.
- Install WordPress or configure WordPress.com site.
- Choose a lightweight free theme (GeneratePress, Astra).
- Install free plugins: Yoast SEO or Rank Math (free), Relevanssi (search), UpdraftPlus (backup), WP RSS Aggregator (RSS ingestion), Cookie consent plugin (Complianz free).
- Deliverable: live or staging site with theme + plugins installed and accessible.

Sprint 2 — Templates & SEO (1 week)
- Create templates for article, guide, category landing pages, homepage block for "In brief" and newsletter CTA.
- Configure SEO: titles, meta, OpenGraph, sitemap (via SEO plugin), and JSON-LD for posts (via plugin or theme hook).
- Deliverable: 3 content templates working.

Sprint 3 — Editorial pipeline & content (1 week)
- Import initial content (10 items: 3 guides, 4 news pieces, 3 practical sheets).
- Configure RSS ingestion for daily news sources.
- Set up newsletter plugin (MailPoet free or Mailchimp free) and subscription form.
- Deliverable: editorial pipeline and 3 published posts.

Sprint 4 — Simple interactive tools (1 week)
- Embed a simple JS calculator (e.g., home insurance premium estimate) as a shortcode.
- Deliverable: working simulator embedded in a post.

Sprint 5 — Legal & launch (1 week)
- Cookie banner and consent management, privacy policy and legal notices pages.
- Basic performance and accessibility checks.
- Deploy live site (WordPress.com or hosting).
- Deliverable: production site with legal notices and daily update pipeline.

Ongoing (daily)
- Daily RSS ingestion and editorial review.
- Publish a daily article or summary.
- Backups and monitoring.

Notes for a free budget
- Prefer free plugins and self-hosted open data (INSEE SIRENE API, data.gouv.fr, RSS feeds).
- Avoid paid APIs (NewsAPI paid tiers, Algolia) — use RSS and open datasets.
