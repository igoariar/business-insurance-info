**In brief**: Choose coverage by checking guarantees (fire, water damage, liability), adapt deductibles, and value your belongings. Compare at least three offers and read exclusions carefully.

## Introduction
Home insurance protects your home and belongings from common risks (fire, water damage, theft). With many offers available, how can you pick the one that really fits your needs? Here is a simple 5-step guide.

### 1. Define precisely what you want to cover
Start by listing your needs: empty or furnished dwelling, value of possessions, any high-value items (jewelry, instruments), and liability coverage. If you rent, check if your lease requires minimum coverage.

### 2. Compare essential guarantees
Basic guarantees include: fire, water damage, theft, broken glass, and liability. Check optional add-ons like legal protection, assistance, or personal accident coverage.

### 3. Check limits and exclusions
Two policies may seem similar but have very different limits (for example, caps on valuable items). Read exclusions carefully — these are situations the insurer will not cover.

### 4. Calculate deductibles and real cost
The deductible is the amount you pay before compensation. A low premium may hide a high deductible. Consider annual cost plus likely deductible in case of a claim.

### 5. Consider service quality and support
Look at claim-handling speed, 24/7 assistance, availability of an internal comparator, and customer reviews. These factors impact your experience in case of an incident.

**FAQ**
- Should I insure valuable items? Often yes, through specific options or higher caps.
- Is switching insurers difficult? Not usually — recent reforms have eased switching, but check terms in each policy.

**Final advice**
Use a reliable comparator and request a detailed quote. Remember: the cheapest option is not always the best if exclusions are extensive.
